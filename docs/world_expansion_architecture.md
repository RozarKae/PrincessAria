## 3. Section 2 Transition Engineering ($x: 2400\text{--}2600\text{px}$)

To ensure the player immediately recognizes they have crossed into an entirely new world without reading a label:

1. **Topographical Drop:**
   - The continuous meadow ground at $y: 880$ ends abruptly at $x: 2460$ in an ancient mossy stone cliff edge.
   - Below $x: 2500$, the bottom terrain plunges into a bottomless mist chasm ($y: 1080+$), demanding platform-to-platform leaps.
2. **Material Shift:**
   - Soft oak boughs give way to massive gnarled redwood roots and bouncy amber nectar rafts (`type: 'honey'`).
   - Climbing vines (`type: 'climbable_vine'`) drop down from canopy boughs, offering new vertical transit.
3. **Lighting & Palette:**
   - Ambient light shifts from open morning glade warmth to dappled canopy shade with deep crepuscular rays.
   - Ambient darkness deepens, illuminated by golden glowing honey rafts and crystal nodes.
4. **Atmospheric Ecology:**
   - Drifting dandelion pollen gives way to heavier tumbling autumn leaves and floating amber spore motes.
   - Distant wind audio transitions to hollow chasm drafts and deep canopy rustles.
5. **Enemy Ecology:**
   - Open ground encounters give way to three-dimensional aerial ambushes over precarious gaps.
