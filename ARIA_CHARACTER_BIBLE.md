## 1. Character Identity & Personality

Princess Aria is the bold, courageous protagonist of *Princess Aria: The Rescue of Batboy*. Rather than waiting in a tower, Aria takes to the hazardous wilderness to rescue Batboy, the mysterious vigilante who once protected her kingdom and is now trapped in the hive fortress of the gigantic Evil Queen Bee.

- **Heroic & Determined:** Resolute in the face of danger, fearless against gigantic insects and towering obstacles.
- **Adventurous & Agile:** Natural platformer athlete; leaps, dashes, climbs, and rolls with grace and stamina.
- **Elegant & Royal:** Maintains royal poise, graceful posture, and refined nobility without being fragile.
- **Compassionate & Curious:** Deeply empathetic toward her allies and fascinated by the secrets of the ancient Honeycomb Glades.
- **Occasionally Playful:** Celebrates victories with a joyful twirl, confident wink, and lighthearted charm.
- **Key Invariant:** She is **NEVER** a passive, helpless damsel. She is the active, driving savior of the story.

## 2. Visual Style & Rendering Direction

- **Style Category:** Premium hand-illustrated HD 2D animated fantasy character.
- **Aesthetic Tone:** Polished cinematic animation with clean silhouette contours, expressive facial features, soft painterly shading, and radiant golden lighting accents.
- **Avoidances:**
  - ❌ **NO Pixel Art:** This is a native Full HD / 4K vector & illustrated hand-crafted visual pipeline.
  - ❌ **NO Generic Anime Tropes:** Expressive, readable eyes with anatomical balance; avoiding oversized bug eyes or hair spires.
  - ❌ **NO Overly Sexualized Features:** Focus on athletic heroism, agility, and elegant adventurous royal tailoring.
  - ❌ **NO Photorealism:** Stylized fantasy art that maintains clarity and charm across all gameplay scales.
  - ❌ **NO Copied Princess Tropes:** Distinctive bee-inspired royal explorer aesthetic unique to Princess Aria.

### 1. Headwear & Royal Crown
- A lightweight, delicate gold circlet with geometric hexagonal honeycomb filigree.
- At the apex brow sits a teardrop **emerald droplet jewel**.
- Kept compact and streamlined so it never distracts or interferes during acrobatics.

### 2. Hair & Styling
- Rich, dark chestnut-brown hair with warm amber and honey-glazed highlights.
- Styled in an **adventurer half-up braid/ponytail** that gathers at the mid-crown and cascades down her back.
- Framing face bangs stay bouncy and react dynamically to wind and horizontal momentum.

### 3. Upper Body & Bodice
- Fitted deep obsidian-charcoal doublet bodice with gold chevron lattice embroidery.
- High scalloped ivory ruffled chemise collar fastened by a hexagonal golden brooch bearing an emerald jewel.
- Gold-trimmed athletic wrist gauntlets protecting hands and wrists during wall-slides and platforming.

### 4. Wing-Cut Royal Capelet
- **CRITICAL RULE:** Aria **DOES NOT** have literal insect wings sprouting from her spine.
- Instead, her royal capelet is tailor-cut from lustrous honey-golden silk in twin swept wing contours.
- In motion, the capelet catches the wind, billows outward, and creates the visual poetry and silhouette of glowing bee wings without literal insect anatomy.

### 5. Skirt & Peplum
- Knee-length layered petal skirt alternating between warm golden amber panels and deep charcoal velvet bands.
- Peplum panniers on the hips create an instantly recognizable hourglass-to-bell silhouette.
- An ivory pleated underskirt peeks out from the hem, providing high contrast against dark platforms.

### 6. Footwear
- Practical knee-high explorer boots crafted from tough charcoal leather.
- Reinforced athletic platforming soles, golden buckle straps, and emerald stitching.
- Flat, sturdy soles designed for vaulting and running (strictly **NO** high heels).

## 5. Master Proportions & Gameplay Scale Test

- **Heroic Game Scale:** Approximately **5.8 heads tall**.
- **Collider Bounds:** Width: `56px`, Height: `84px` (Crouch Height: `54px`).
- **Visual HD Render Bounds:** Width: `138px`, Height: `138px` centered on foot anchor `(0.5, 0.95)`.

### Scale Readability Verification
The character has been designed and tested across standard platformer display sizes:
- **32px Height:** High-contrast color blocks (Gold, Charcoal, Ivory) and capelet silhouette remain 100% distinguishable from background geometry.
- **48px Height:** Skirt peplum, hair braid, and boot cuffs clearly readable.
- **64px Height:** Facial eye direction, bodice chevron lines, and capelet movement visible.
- **96px Height:** Tiara geometry, emerald jewel, and ivory collar ruffles distinct.
- **128px Height & Above:** Micro-embroidery, eye highlights, soft blush, and painterly fabric gradients fully expressed.

## 9. HD Asset Pipeline & Source Resolution Strategy

- **Master High-Res Source Resolution:** All master production assets are authored at **`2048 × 2048 px`** (or clean scalable SVG vectors).
- **In-Engine Resolution Scaling:** The game renderer dynamically renders artwork down into native Full HD (1080p) or Quad HD (1440p) logical buffers using `window.devicePixelRatio`.
- **Scaling Filter:** Strict enforcement of `ctx.imageSmoothingEnabled = true` and `ctx.imageSmoothingQuality = 'high'` prevents any pixelation, blurring, or jagged edges.
- **Supported File Formats:**
  - `SVG`: Infinite vector scalability for clean lines, UI, and master sheets.
  - `PNG` (32-bit with Alpha): High-fidelity illustrated textures and raster frames.
  - `WebP`: Highly compressed, lossless raster delivery for production web distribution.

## 10. Invariant Design Rules (Do NOT Violate)

1. **NO Insect Wings on Aria:** Aria is human. Her costume references bees through golden/charcoal tailoring, hexagonal patterns, and her wing-cut silk capelet. Do not attach wings to her back.
2. **5-Color Dominance:** Always maintain the Golden Yellow primary, Obsidian Charcoal secondary, Ivory Cream highlights, Emerald accents, and Chestnut-Amber hair.
3. **Small Practical Tiara:** The tiara is a platformer crown — delicate, geometric, and securely seated with its center emerald droplet. It must never look like a cumbersome royal coronation crown.
4. **Explorer Footwear:** Boots must always be knee-high, flat-soled, reinforced platforming boots. Never substitute slippers or heels.
5. **Heroic Silhouette:** Aria's posture must remain athletic, agile, and courageous across every animation.
